# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Young urban streetwear buyers in LATAM (Colombia, priced in COP). They're scrolling Instagram, discovering drops through word of mouth, and buying with attitude. They want pieces that speak for themselves — no logos screaming, no hypebeast clichés. They're 18-30, style-conscious, and value authenticity over brands.

## Product Purpose
HitHats sells gorras (caps) and sacos (oversized jackets) for people who move through the city with intention. The site is a single collection landing page (COL. 001) — discover, vibe, buy. No account walls, no friction. The goal is to make the product feel inevitable: this isn't shopping, it's self-expression.

## Brand Personality
Rebellious, loud, authentic. The voice is direct, Spanish, and unapologetic — "No Pidas Permiso" (Don't Ask Permission). It speaks TO the customer, not AT them. Think street corner energy, not boardroom polish.

## Anti-references
AI slop: warm cream/sand backgrounds, glassmorphism, gradient text, generic SaaS hero-metrics, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, numbered section markers (01/02/03), identical card grids, bounce easing, gray-on-color text. Any design that screams "I used a template."

## Design Principles
1. **Show, don't sell** — Product images and motion do the talking; copy is minimal and punchy.
2. **Earn the scroll** — Every section justifies its existence through visual impact, not filler.
3. **Street is the standard** — Design decisions pass the "would this fly on a wall in Medellín?" test.
4. **Dark by default** — Black backgrounds aren't edgy; they're honest. The product is the light source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Alt text on all product images. Keyboard-navigable menu and product links. No content gated on hover-only interactions.
